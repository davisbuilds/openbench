Important caveats

  • Harness mode is not apples-to-apples: aider ran as a one-shot --message invocation, while pi and opencode ran full agentic loops. Token comparison reflects that difference in harness mode, not a pure like-for-like agent loop contest.
  • One aider cell excluded: 1 of 9 aider cells was infrastructure-classified and is excluded from solve-rate denominators (aider reported as 8/8). pi and opencode are 9/9.
  • Fresh tokens only: figures are proxy-measured fresh tokens (uncached input + output). Cache reads are shown separately and are not included. Honest comparison: 5,856 vs 8,184 vs 14,186 fresh tokens/solve (aider vs pi vs opencode).
  • Verifiable bundle: this release includes results.jsonl and provenance.json. Re-check digests with obench verify docs/releases/2026-07-20-aider-showcase.

Methodology & limitations

What this card is

A self-contained OpenBench comparison bundle: candidate harness aider (BYO manifest) highlighted against stock arms pi and opencode on the same task set and model.

What obench verify proves

  • The bundled results.jsonl still matches the SHA-256 recorded in provenance.json (tamper-evident).
  • When local task trees are available, per-task content digests (instruction.md + checker.sh + workspace / workspace.toml) still match.

What verify does NOT prove

  • Runs were not cherry-picked or rerun until green.
  • Auth material, secrets, or LOCAL-ONLY transcripts were reviewed (transcripts are never included in the bundle).
  • Candidate admission-gate live checks were executed on this machine.
  • That one-shot vs agentic-loop token totals are interchangeable product metrics — they are not.
