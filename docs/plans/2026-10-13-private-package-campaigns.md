  trust its evaluator as host code. Task metadata never grants that authority.
- Export only the solver projection into a sealed Harbor task. Keep the archive,
  hidden cases, controls and evaluator outside the solver environment.

  evidence, fresh quality replay and atomic result import. Preserve old readers.
- Reuse existing quality gates, including two distinct valid implementations,
  targeted defects and authoritative replay; diagnostic replay cannot admit runs.
- Prove actual harness allowed edits/build/browser operations and denied direct,
  symlink and subprocess reads of known-present private assets on the execution
  host. Authoring context is never solver context.
