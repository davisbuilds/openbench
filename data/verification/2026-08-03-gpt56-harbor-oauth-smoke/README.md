# Harbor OAuth end-to-end verification evidence

This directory is repository-internal validation and reproducibility evidence
for the Harbor OAuth execution path. It was produced by `obench publish`, but
it is intentionally **not** a finalized public benchmark release and must not
be copied under `docs/releases/` or listed in `docs/releases.json`.

The small smoke matrix proves the execution and evidence pipeline, not a
general harness ranking. Keeping the sealed bundle under `data/verification/`
allows future contributors to re-run its offline integrity checks without
placing provisional results on the live static site.

## Re-verify

```bash
obench verify .
```

`obench verify` recomputes the SHA-256 of `results.jsonl` and, when task
trees are available locally, per-task content digests recorded in
`provenance.json`. Each check prints PASS or FAIL.

### What verify proves

- The results file has not been edited since publish (hash match).
- Task content digests still match the recorded instruction/checker/workspace
  hashes when those tasks are present on the verifying machine.

### What verify does NOT prove

- The runs were not cherry-picked or rerun until green.
- Live candidate-gate checks succeeded on the publisher's machine.
- Transcripts were reviewed (they are **never** included — LOCAL-ONLY).

Do not publish this smoke bundle as a benchmark result. A separate release may
be finalized only after its publication decision and complete result scope are
explicitly recorded in `docs/releases.json` with `"status": "final"`.
